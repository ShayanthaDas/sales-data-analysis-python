# Sales Data Analysis – Case Study

## 1. Background

Sales data is one of the most common types of business data. It helps understand what products are selling, how revenue is generated, and where improvements can be made.

This project uses Python to analyze sales data and find useful patterns.

---

## 2. Problem

The main questions for this analysis were:

* Which products are generating the most sales?
* Which categories are performing well?
* How do sales change over time?
* Are there any weak-performing products?

The goal was to answer these using data analysis.

---

## 3. Dataset

The dataset includes:

* Product name
* Category
* Sales amount
* Quantity sold
* Order date
* Customer or region information (if available)

Some cleaning was needed before analysis.

---

## 4. Approach

### Data cleaning

* Removed missing values
* Fixed inconsistent formats
* Standardized column names

### Analysis

Focused on:

* Total sales by product
* Category-wise performance
* Time-based trends
* Top and bottom performers

### Visualization

Used charts to understand patterns in:

* Sales distribution
* Product performance
* Category comparison
* Trend over time

---

## 5. Findings

### Sales concentration

Most revenue comes from a small group of products.

---

### Category differences

Some categories consistently outperform others in sales.

---

### Time trends

Sales fluctuate over time and are not evenly distributed.

---

### Low-performing products exist

Some products consistently show low sales and may need review.

---

## 6. Business insights

The analysis shows that focusing on a few strong products and categories can improve overall performance.

Tracking sales trends regularly can help in better planning and decision-making.

---

## 7. Recommendations

* Focus on high-performing products
* Improve or review weak categories
* Monitor sales trends regularly
* Use data to guide product decisions

---

## 8. Conclusion

This project shows how Python can be used to turn raw sales data into simple but useful business insights.

It also helped me improve my understanding of basic data analysis workflow.

---

## 9. What I learned

* How to use Python for data analysis
* How to clean and structure real datasets
* How to find patterns in business data
* How to present insights in a simple way
